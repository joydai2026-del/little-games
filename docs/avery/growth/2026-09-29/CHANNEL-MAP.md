## Compliance note on cold email (CAN-SPAM)
CAN-SPAM covers commercial email to anyone, B2B included. It requires no deceptive headers or subject lines, an ad disclosure, a valid physical postal address, a working opt-out honored within 10 business days, and responsibility for any vendor that sends for you. Penalties are per email. The bigger practical risks: many school district filters quarantine unsolicited vendor mail; scraping staff directories or the MIPC list goes against their terms (MIPC says non-commercial only); and state privacy laws plus district vendor policies may apply. JD also prefers inbound (AEO + Message Followers + on-site list). **Rank: last. Don't do it.** Personal, one-to-one notes to organizations that publish a resource-submission address (CELIN, MIPC) are not bulk cold email, but they still need JD's approval to send.
