# Should we spin up a dedicated outreach/community agent? (2026-09-29)

**Recommendation: yes, but make it a "drafts + research" agent with no sending authority.** Run it daily at about 11:00 AM ET, after the 9:32 daily-ops sweep, and cap it at 20 minutes. Its job is to feed JD a short approval queue, not to post.

## What it does daily (no approval needed)
1. **Pinterest queue:** render 2–3 fresh pin images and copy (new headline/variant) for the current seasonal SKUs plus 1 guide page, and add them to `reports/pins-queue/YYYY-MM-DD/`. If JD later connects a Pinterest business account and explicitly grants standing permission for pins to our own account, it schedules them. Until then it only drafts.
2. **AEO/SEO:** one guide or FAQ improvement PR a week on joydong.org (next: CNY, Thanksgiving, stroke order K-2); check that the guides are indexed (site: queries) and whether ChatGPT/Perplexity cite averystudio.org for 5 tracked questions, logged weekly.
3. **Community listening:** read public posts only (MIPC news, CELIN, CLASS events, CLTA/ACTFL calls for proposals, r/ChineseLanguage new questions about kids/stroke order) and draft replies to the 1–3 best-fit questions in `reports/community-queue/`.
4. **Waitlist hygiene:** count waitlist emails (once a list provider exists, read-only), and draft the weekly Message Followers note for the ops run to send.
5. **Target list upkeep:** keep `OUTREACH-TARGETS.csv` current (rules changes, new groups JD found while logged in).

## What always needs JD's approval (the agent never does these)
- Any post, comment, or DM **as JD** in Facebook groups, Reddit, Google Groups, Xiaohongshu, Instagram, or YouTube.
- Any email to an organization or person (MIPC, CELIN, CLASS, CSAUS/NCACLS, Utah DLI, peer sellers).
- Creating accounts (Pinterest, Search Console, list provider), spending money (conference, ads, memberships), and pricing changes.
- Using any contact list, and never scraping MIPC/district lists.

## Already covered by existing authority (not the new agent)
- TPT listing edits, freebies, and the weekly Message Followers send stay with the daily-ops/promo runs.

## Why not full autonomy
FB groups and subreddits ban accounts for automated or promotional posting, and a ban on JD's personal account would cost more than any single post earns. The volume is small (a few posts a week), so JD approving a queue on her phone takes about 5 minutes a day.

## Success metrics (review Oct 31)
- 50 founding-teacher waitlist sign-ups
- TPT followers from 6 to 25
- ≥10 Pinterest outbound clicks/week
- ≥1 AI-answer citation of averystudio.org for a tracked question
- October TPT earnings > September
