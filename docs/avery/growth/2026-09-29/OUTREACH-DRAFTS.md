Parents of K-2 Mandarin immersion kids: what's working for you for 10-minute character practice at home?

Here's what works in my classroom (I teach K-5 Chinese): (1) pick only this week's 5–6 words, (2) say it, trace it, find it in a picture, and (3) end with a 2-minute game so it's not a worksheet fight.

## D9. r/ChineseLanguage: helpful-answer template (reply only to a relevant question; no link unless asked)
> For young kids learning stroke order, what worked in my K-5 classes: learn the 8 basic strokes first (横 竖 撇 捺 点 提 钩 折), trace each new character 3 times in a 田字格 while saying the stroke names, then do a quick "missing stroke" game where one stroke is left out and they add it. Keep it to 5–6 characters a week. (I make teaching materials, so happy to share a link if anyone wants one.)
