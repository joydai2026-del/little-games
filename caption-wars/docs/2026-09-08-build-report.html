Build report for JJ, 2026-09-08. Repo joydai2026-del/little-games (private), PR #1. Play: caption-wars.joyd-ai-2026.workers.dev

What you asked for: one photo drops, everyone captions, vote the winner. Web page, phone-first, public link, humans and AI players mixed, random internet photo.

What you have: exactly that, deployed, reviewed nine times by two different models, played live many times over the API, in a phone-sized browser on two tabs, and by Claude from the terminal.

The one catch today: the free Cloudflare AI allowance (10,000 "neurons" a day) was spent by the prompt-tuning runs, so until it resets the AI players sit out and the game says so plainly. Humans can play without them. Three decisions below are yours.

How to play

  • Open the link on your phone, type a name, tap Create a room. Share the 4-character code or the link.
  • Friends open the same link and join with the code. You pick rounds, timers and how many AI players (0 to 4).
  • Each round: photo, write a caption, vote for someone else's, see who wrote what and who won. Champion at the end.
  • Let Claude play from your Mac: export CAPTION_WARS_URL=<worker url> && node caption-wars/agent/play.mjs --url $CAPTION_WARS_URL --room CODE --name Claude --brain claude (also --brain codex; grok plays without seeing the photo).

Three decisions for you

1. Pay Cloudflare $5 a month for Workers Paid?
Free tier: 10,000 neurons a day. A 5-round game with 2 AI players costs roughly 300, so about 30 games a day for free. Tuning the bot prompt (npm run ai:try) costs several games per run and is what spent today's allowance. Paid: the same 10,000 included, then about $0.011 per 1,000 more. Default I set: stay free, never tune on a game day.
2. Which photo tags?
Reviewers pulled real photos from the tags and found party and costume return sexualised close-ups (3 of 7), and statue returned a full-frontal museum nude (1 of 7). I removed those three. Current list: dog, cat, funny, awkward, baby, goat, fail, duck, pigeon, squirrel, cake. Anything with people in it is your call; adding a tag back is one line in wrangler.jsonc.
3. Are the AI player names and voices right?
Daisy Deadpan, Chaos Chip, Sunny Wholesome, Dramatic Rex, each with a one-line joke style. Rename or rewrite in src/shared/personas.ts.

What protects your friends

  • Bot captions are checked twice: a word-list guard for labelling people by race, ethnicity, religion or disability (principle written into the code so it stops being re-argued), then a second model judges "is this a caption or the AI talking about itself". A bad answer is regenerated once, then a fallback model is tried, then the bot sits the round out.
  • Human captions are never filtered; they are shown as plain text (no HTML), so a pasted script is just text.
  • Nobody can vote twice, vote for themselves, see authors before the reveal, or act as another player (per-player secret on every request).
  • A phone that sleeps through a round comes back on the right screen. A stalled photo host or model has a timeout everywhere.

Not verified yet (honest list)

  • The three builds since round 6 have not had a bot write a caption in front of a reviewer, because the AI allowance was spent. First thing after the reset: play one game with bots and read every caption.
  • The "AI ran out mid-round" path is proven in unit tests and by reading the deployed code, not observed live (it needs the allowance to run out mid-game).
  • No real phone yet, only a phone-sized browser. Keyboard-over-Send on iOS and the clipboard fallback are unproven.
  • The room-create rate limit does not stop a concurrent burst. Harmless on the free plan; must be fixed before a paid plan (written in the plan as rule 69).
